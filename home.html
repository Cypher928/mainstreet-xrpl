How it works

From lease to recovery in three steps

No implementation project. Upload what you already have and MainStreet does the reading.

STEP 01
↑

Upload

Drop in leases, CAM statements and vendor invoices — including scanned copier PDFs. MainStreet handles the file so you don't have to think about format or size.

STEP 02
✦

AI analysis

Lease terms, caps and exclusions are extracted and reviewed. Every charge is checked against what the lease actually permits — with a confidence score and a human review gate.

STEP 03

Recover revenue

Under-billed recoveries surface with the evidence attached. Generate the tenant statement, resolve disputes, and settle — with the full trail preserved.
