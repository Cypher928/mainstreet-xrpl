05 Why XRPL

Not bolted on — it's the audit layer.

A wire moves money but can't prove what for. An RLUSD payment carries a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact reconciliation in its memo — anyone can verify it, on infrastructure no one party controls.

  • RLUSDDollar settlement. A regulated stablecoin — no volatility, the currency CAM is actually owed in.
  • FAST3–5 second finality at sub-cent fees. No settlement float, no chargebacks.
  • PROOFPublicly verifiable. Landlord, tenant, and auditor check the same ledger entry.
  • PRIVSensitive lease data stays off-chain. Only a one-way hash goes on-ledger — never names or amounts.
