# MainStreet — Make Waves Demo Kit: 7-Minute Script

Exact clicks, talking points, the strongest path, and the do-not-click list.
Built against the current preview branch (feature-frozen demo build).

**Golden rule:** every number you say out loud is already on screen. Never
promise what the next click shows — click, then narrate what appeared.

---

## Pre-demo checklist (do this 30 minutes before)

1. Log in as your landlord account; confirm the **AI Command Center** loads
   with the demo data (Cascade Commons seeded; Harborview in Acquisitions).
2. Open livenet.xrpl.org in a **second browser tab, pre-loaded** with the
   settlement transaction — never type a URL live.
3. Zoom browser to 110–125% so the back row can read cards.
4. Close every other tab; hide bookmarks bar; turn off notifications.
5. Do a full silent run-through once. If any number differs from this script,
   update your talking points to match the screen — not the other way around.
6. Have one rehearsed lease PDF on the desktop ONLY if you plan the optional
   extraction segment (see 6:00 block) — and have already uploaded that exact
   file successfully at least twice.

## The 7 minutes

### 0:00–0:50 — Open on the Command Center (the hook)

**Click:** nothing. You're already on ✨ AI Command Center.

**Say:**
> "Every year, commercial landlords bill tenants for shared operating costs —
> CAM reconciliation. It runs on spreadsheets and misread leases, and it's so
> error-prone that an entire consulting industry exists just to audit the
> mistakes. This is MainStreet — an AI asset manager for that money. This is
> a real portfolio state, computed live: [read the screen] one property,
> $99,542 of identified value, 4 priorities today, and an executive summary
> written by deterministic analysis — not a chatbot guessing."

**Point at** (don't click): the executive summary line about the settlement —
> "…and notice this line: 'the reconciled settlement is verified on the XRP
> Ledger.' Hold that thought — I'll prove it on the public ledger in a minute."

### 0:50–1:50 — One priority card, end to end (Phase 29 deep links)

**Click:** on the *"lease has expired"* high-priority card, the **Open lease
review** button.

**Say (while it navigates):**
> "Every recommendation answers four things: what's wrong, why it matters in
> dollars, what to do next — and this button takes me to the exact item.
> Not a dashboard. The actual field."

You land in the AI Review Workspace, scrolled to the highlighted **Lease End**
field with its confidence and source document.

> "Here's the expired lease term — with the confidence score and the source
> document it was read from. Every field MainStreet extracts keeps a verbatim
> quote and page number from the lease. That evidence follows the number
> everywhere. Nothing here is a black box."

**Click:** close the review workspace (X).

### 1:50–3:00 — The money engine (CAM tab)

**Click:** the **CAM** tab.

**Say:**
> "26 invoices for the year, categorized. MainStreet allocates them across
> five leases — pro-rata shares, expense caps, exclusions — the math every
> landlord does by hand in Excel over two weeks. Here it's a compute."

**Scroll** to the allocation results table. **Click one tenant row** to expand.

> "Per tenant: their share, their cap applied, and the lease evidence behind
> each term. This expansion is what a tenant sees on their statement — which
> is why disputes get resolved against documents instead of arguments."

**Scroll** to Open Disputes.
> "Two live disputes. When I resolve one, the decision is recorded with a
> SHA-256 audit hash. Every money decision in this product leaves a
> fingerprint. Keep that word in mind — fingerprint."

(Do NOT resolve one live — the resolved state persists and your next
rehearsal starts dirty. Just point.)

### 3:00–4:15 — Ask the portfolio anything (AI Workspace)

**Click:** ✨ **AI Workspace** (top nav). **Click** the suggestion chip
**"Explain Cascade Commons's reconciliation"** (or type it).

**Say:**
> "This is not an LLM improvising. It's a deterministic engine — the same
> computation as that table, with a reasoning trace. Same input, same answer,
> every time. In a product that produces legal invoices, that's the only AI
> a CFO can sign off on."

**Click:** "How I got this answer" (the reasoning trace) — expand it briefly.

**Click:** a **citation chip** on the answer.
> "And every claim opens the actual document at the cited page, quote
> highlighted. We call it the Evidence Viewer. Our AI doesn't ask for trust —
> it shows receipts."

**Close** the viewer.

### 4:15–5:45 — The XRPL moment (this wins or loses it)

**Click:** back to the property **Overview** tab. **Scroll** to the RLUSD
settlement stepper (green, verified).

**Say:**
> "The reconciliation says the tenants owe this landlord a specific number.
> Here's the part no spreadsheet can do: settlement in RLUSD — Ripple's
> regulated dollar stablecoin — on XRPL mainnet. Not a testnet. Real value,
> 3-second finality, sub-cent fees."

**Click:** **View Transaction ↗** (or switch to the pre-loaded explorer tab).

> "This is livenet.xrpl.org — public infrastructure we don't control. Look at
> two fields. The Source Tag, 2606290001 — that's MainStreet's traffic,
> identifiable on-ledger. And the memo: a SHA-256 fingerprint of the exact
> reconciliation this payment settles. Anyone holding the reconciliation data
> can recompute that hash. Nobody — including us — can pair this payment with
> a different invoice after the fact. The ledger isn't decoration here. It's
> the audit layer: lease evidence → deterministic math → on-chain proof.
> That chain of custody for a dollar amount is the product."

**Switch back** to the app tab.

### 5:45–6:30 — Acquisition intelligence (breadth in 45 seconds)

**Click:** **Portfolio** → in Acquisitions, open **Harborview Retail Center**.

**Say:**
> "Same engines, pointed at a building you don't own yet. Upload the rent
> roll during due diligence: CAM recovery rate, cap leakage, revenue at risk
> — an investment answer in minutes instead of an analyst-week. Landlords buy
> buildings based on numbers like these; now the numbers come with evidence."

### 6:30–7:00 — Close on the Command Center

**Click:** back to the **AI Command Center**.

**Say:**
> "So: AI that reads leases with page-cited evidence. A deterministic engine
> that computes the money. And XRPL that settles it with proof a bank wire
> can't produce. CAM reconciliation is a $100-billion-a-year blind spot in
> commercial real estate, run on spreadsheets. MainStreet makes every dollar
> of it traceable — from the sentence in the lease to the hash on the ledger.
> That's MainStreet. Thank you."

## Strongest demo path (summary)

Command Center → priority card deep-link → Review Workspace (evidence) →
CAM tab (allocation + dispute pointing) → AI Workspace (explain + citation →
Evidence Viewer) → Overview settlement stepper → **livenet explorer** →
Harborview acquisition → Command Center close.

It's strongest because it mirrors the value chain: *find the problem → prove
the data → compute the money → settle with proof* — and it ends on XRPL and
the daily-driver screen, which is what Make Waves judges score.

## What NOT to click (each of these is a rehearsal-tested rule)

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| **Reserves tab on Cascade Commons** | The demo property has no reserve documents — you'll show an empty state. Reserve Intelligence is only demoable if you've pre-uploaded a mortgage PDF and rehearsed it. |
| **"Retry" on any failed lease job** | Known crash path in the current build (it's on the fix list, but the build is frozen). |
| **Reset/Delete tenant controls** | A known index bug can remove the wrong tenant from persisted data — this would corrupt your demo seed live. |
| **"Calculate CAM Charges" (re-run)** | It recomputes and can shift the numbers you've memorized; the allocation modal also eats 30+ seconds. The results are already on screen. |
| **GL Excel upload / Yardi import** | Requires a correctly-formatted file; the error path shows a generic message. Nothing about a CSV upload wins a hackathon. |
| **Resolve a dispute live** | The resolution persists; your next run-through inherits a dirty state. Point at the buttons, don't press them. |
| **An unrehearsed lease PDF** | Extraction latency and variance on stage. If you want the extraction wow-moment, use your twice-tested PDF, start it, and narrate the Command Center while it processes. |
| **Sign out / role switching / tenant portal** | Requires a second account and breaks flow. Mention the tenant portal verbally at the dispute section instead. |
| **▶ Guided Tour** | It's for onboarding; it takes control of the screen away from you. |
| **Print/CSV export buttons** | Popup windows + demo-machine popup blockers = dead air. |
| **Browser refresh** | Safe (persistence works), but costs 10 dead seconds and reruns seed checks. Don't do it to "fix" anything mid-demo — navigate instead. |

## Confusing UX — don't demonstrate these

- **Three ways to import expenses** (GL Excel vs invoice upload vs Yardi CSV)
  — pick zero of them; the invoices are already in.
- **The review-queue filter tabs** — if all demo tenants are verified, the
  panel is hidden and hunting for it looks like a bug. The deep-link from the
  Command Center card is the reliable route into review.
- **Confidence percentages** — fine on screen, but don't invite "how exactly
  is 92% computed?" by reading them aloud. Say "high confidence, and here's
  the evidence" and click the evidence instead. The evidence is the answer.
- **The "Coming Soon" disabled state** — honest design, but don't point a
  judge at what doesn't exist.
- **Multiple browser windows for reports** — the print-window pattern is
  functional, not beautiful. Reports are a talking point, not a click.

## If something breaks live

- Extraction hangs → "That's a live AI call — while it thinks, let me show
  you what it produces" → switch to an already-extracted tenant's evidence.
- Explorer tab fails → you pre-loaded it; if the venue network dies, say the
  hash out loud and show the app's stepper — "verifiable by anyone, anytime."
- Wrong screen → the ✨ AI Command Center nav button is your reset button.
  Never refresh.
